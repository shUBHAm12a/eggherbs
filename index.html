An egg is never just an egg.

The Egg & Herb is built on one idea: take the street-food flavours of Surat and give the humble egg the main role. Boiled, half-fried, folded into an omelette or simmered in gravy.


Each dish leans on fresh herbs and a hot tadka at the end. Mains arrive with buns, because good gravy deserves something to carry it.
